FAQ

What is Pixel Candles?

1,111 pixel-art candlesticks on Robinhood Chain: 500 green, 500 red and 111 rare gold. Each one is a chart candle and a real candle at once, flame included.

What shape will I get?

One of five chart shapes: Classic, Hammer, Star, Doji or Marubozu. The shape is decided by the candle's token number, so you see it as soon as you mint.

Is the chart a real price?

No. The chart at the top is decoration that prints pixel candles. Mint prices are fixed and read from the contract.

What do I need to mint?

A browser wallet with ETH on Robinhood Chain, enough for the mint price plus a small network fee. Up to 10 per transaction.

Where does the money go?

Every mint payment goes to the creator. Mints are final and there are no refunds.

Can I lose money?

Yes. A mint cannot be refunded, and a candle may later be worth less than you paid, or nothing. Only spend what you can afford to lose.
